Why do people love flowers?

Flowers are used as a communal language to show love, support, and thankfulness to other people. Whether it be for special occasions, to apologize, or simply just because. Everyone enjoys receiving flowers. They bring happiness visually and can help relieve stress aromatically.

Similar to the birth stones, every month of the year has a primary and secondary flower that is associated with it. These flowers are based on what time of the year they are in bloom.
